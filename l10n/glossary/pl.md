Source: git's official Polish `po` translation conventions (git keeps most
porcelain terms as English loanwords in common Polish developer usage) and
VS Code's Polish language pack terminology. Loanwords used by Polish
developers day to day are kept in English, matching both git's own Polish
output and VS Code's Polish UI.
